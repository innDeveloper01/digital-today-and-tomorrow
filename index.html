About the Project
01

Introduction

This is the project about digital tools that can be used in most of the lessons to increase the motivation, understanding and attraction of students for the lesson topics. Digitalisation is inevitable today. It makes our life easier and faster in most of the areas. By using technology in the right and correct way, we can make our lessons more enjoyable, concrete and productive. We can learn, teach, do homework or follow all kinds of innovations and developments in so many areas, both inside or outside of the school.

02

Project Rules

Our Goal: To use technology responsibly, creatively, and effectively to enhance our learning and collaboration.

  • The tool should help us achieve our learning goal — it shouldn't be used for entertainment or fun only.
  • We are respectful, responsible, and kind online, just as we are in the classroom. Our digital footprint matters.
  • We never share personal information (full name, address, phone number, passwords) online as part of this project.
  • Before saying "it doesn't work," we will try to solve the problem. We will check our internet, restart the app, or ask our teachers or classmates for help.
  • We will use the digital tool only for its intended educational purpose. We will not browse other websites, play games, or do anything unrelated to the task.
03

Aims of the Project

🎯

To learn suitable digital tools that can be used in and outside of the classroom to enhance and increase the motivation of students.

💡

To concretize abstract concepts using digital tools so that students can understand these subjects more easily.

✅

To show students some ways to use technology correctly and effectively.

04

Expected Results

  • Partner teachers and students will learn to use some digital tools effectively in their lessons.
  • The success rate and motivation of students will increase by using digital tools.
  • Pupils will learn a few ways of using technology in the right and effective way.
  • We will create a Twinspace, public Instagram and Facebook pages to share our work.
  • We will create a common product: E-book, E-blog, and a website.
  • By having contact with other pupils domestic or abroad, the communication and language skills of the students will develop.
  • By completing the tasks in this project, the awareness of responsibility will develop.
  • Partners will learn the awareness and importance of belonging and being part of a task by fulfilling their part in common product work.
